Plugin and entries
Enable the plugin, then choose the status of new entries, their owner and how imported tags are transformed.
Public update
Feeds can be updated when a visitor loads a page (before or after display, or through Ajax). With Ajax, your theme must have the behavior publicHeadContent. Keep the number of feeds updated at one time to 1, a large number may cause a timeout.
Public side
Enable the public page of sources, choose where entry titles link to the original entry and where the full content is shown.
Requirement
A writable cache folder is required.
